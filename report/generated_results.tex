\section{Discussion}
Compute-matched comparisons separate presentation order from additional optimisation. Fold-level intervals and Holm-adjusted tests are interpreted as exploratory because outer training folds overlap.
\section{Conclusion}
For dry\_bean, fixed and dynamic incremental effects relative to their compute-matched baselines were +0.0011 and -0.0015, respectively. 
For steel\_plates, fixed and dynamic incremental effects relative to their compute-matched baselines were +0.0101 and -0.0276, respectively.
